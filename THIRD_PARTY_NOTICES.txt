GW2 Keyboard for Nexus – third-party components shipped with the add-on
=====================================================================

1. whisper.cpp and ggml (GW2Keyboard\voice\whisper.dll, ggml*.dll – the voice input), version 1.9.5
   https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp
   Licence: MIT (full text below), Copyright (c) 2023-2026 The ggml authors

2. Whisper speech recognition models (ggml-*.bin, downloaded on request into the "models" folder)
   OpenAI Whisper weights converted for whisper.cpp: https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp
   Licence: MIT, Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI – https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/LICENSE
   The files are checked against their SHA-256 before they are used.

3. Compiled into GW2Chatlator.dll (formerly gw2keyboard_nexus.dll):
   Nexus API header (Nexus.h), Copyright (c) 2023 Raidcore.GG – https://github.com/RaidcoreGG/RCGG-lib-nexus-api
   Dear ImGui 1.80, Copyright (c) 2014-2021 Omar Cornut – https://github.com/ocornut/imgui
   Licence: MIT (full texts in addons/nexus-keyboard/third_party of the source code)

4. Unicode CLDR 49 (Common Locale Data Repository): the names of languages in the window's languages,
   compiled into GW2Chatlator.dll (src/core/language_names.inc, generated by tools/gen_language_names.py)
   https://cldr.unicode.org – Licence: Unicode License V3 (full text below), Copyright (c) 1991-2026 Unicode, Inc.
